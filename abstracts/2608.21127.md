# BMPV black holes in higher-derivative supergravity

**Authors:** Yide Cai, Sabarenath Jayaprakash, James T. Liu, Yi Pang, Robert J. Saskowski

There are five independent four-derivative superinvariants for the five-dimensional STU model, of which two are vector invariants that do not involve curvature tensors. We construct the corrected BMPV black hole directly in five dimensions with this set of general four-derivative couplings, a computation made possible with the help of AI. We find that the vector invariants do not correct the solution or the entropy, while the entropy associated with the heterotic corrections agrees with recent results.
