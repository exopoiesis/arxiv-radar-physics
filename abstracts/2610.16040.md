# CLNO: Conservative Local Neural Operator for Compressible Flow Prediction

**Authors:** Buxuan Wang, Rongzu Li, Qingsong Zou

We propose a conservative local neural operator (CLNO) for accurate and generalizable autoregressive prediction of compressible flows. Building upon the locality and translation invariance of the local neural operator (LNO), CLNO introduces a conservative evolution framework based on local subdomains of fixed physical size, separating local mean-state evolution from fine-scale reconstruction. The flow is encoded into subdomain-integrated conservative states and fine-scale features, while a shared local network predicts interface fluxes for finite-volume updates that enforce discrete conservation. A convolutional reconstruction network recovers fine-scale structures using zero-mean basis functions, preserving the updated local means and providing information for subsequent evolution. To accommodate different physical parameters, Reynolds--Mach number conditioning modulates the encoding, flux-prediction, and reconstruction networks, enabling a single model to learn parameter-dependent dynamics and generalize to unseen parameter combinations without retraining. CLNO supports prediction across spatial resolutions, computational domain sizes, and boundary conditions. Numerical experiments demonstrate improved generalization and competitive or higher accuracy compared with LNO, including order-of-magnitude error reductions in representative cases, at approximately three to five times the inference cost on periodic benchmarks. These results demonstrate accurate, generalizable, and physically consistent flow prediction through conservative local evolution and fine-scale reconstruction.
