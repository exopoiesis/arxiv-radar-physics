# jax-SNANA: A GPU-accelerated Supernova Framework for the SBI Era

**Authors:** Matthew Grayling

We present jax-SNANA, a JAX reimplementation of the supernova analysis framework SNANA. jax-SNANA enables GPU-accelerated transient simulations and light curve fitting, supernova standardisation and cosmological inference. Running in single precision on an NVIDIA A100 GPU, jax-SNANA simulates more than 100,000 LSST-observed supernovae per second and fits 5,000 light curves per second after JIT compilation, a two orders-of-magnitude increase over current tools, essential for analysing samples at the scale expected from LSST. jax-SNANA currently supports the SALT and BayeSN spectral energy distribution (SED) models as well as physical models from redback-jax and a variety of SN SED templates; light curve fitting is supported with both photometric and spectroscopic redshifts. We fully validate the accuracy of jax-SNANA by comparing to SNANA, finding excellent consistency between the two across a variety of tasks. The code provides a modular JAX end-to-end supernova cosmology pipeline, user-extensible to support new features including different SED templates and photometric classification algorithms. jax-SNANA has been designed with future applications of simulation-based inference in mind, providing fast simulations and enabling fully compiled on-the-fly simulations to be generated within the training loop of downstream SBI tasks. jax-SNANA extends beyond SNANA by supporting simulations of generic SN-host galaxy relationships and multiple sub-populations of events, for example originating from young and old progenitors. jax-SNANA is publicly available at [https://github.com/mattgrayling/jax-snana](https://github.com/mattgrayling/jax-snana) and installable through pip.
