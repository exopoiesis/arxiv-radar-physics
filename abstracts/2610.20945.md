# Bounds on hadronic contributions to the anomalous magnetic moment of the $τ$ lepton from QCD Sum Rules

**Authors:** Siyuan Li, J. Ho, R. T. Kleiv, D. Leighton, E. J. Obbink, T. G. Steele

A QCD sum-rule study is performed to place bounds on the leading-order (LO) hadronic vacuum polarization (HVP) contribution to the anomalous magnetic moment of the $τ$ lepton ($a_τ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO},\, a_τ= (g-2)_τ/2$). The QCD contributions to $a_τ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO}$ are modeled as a combination of Laplace QCD sum rules (LSRs) and finite-energy QCD sum rules (FESRs) of various weights, which are bounded by Hölder and related fundamental inequalities. The light-quark ($u,\,d,\,s$) contributions are evaluated up to five-loop order in perturbative contributions in the chiral limit, LO in light quark mass corrections, next-to-leading order (NLO) in dimension-four QCD condensates and to LO in dimension-six QCD condensates. Extending previous work on $a_μ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO}$, we present a robust methodology for using QCD sum rules to constrain $a_τ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO}$, and more generally any lepton $g-2$. A criterion is developed to extract from the bounds analysis a final result of $a_τ^\mathrm{QCD} = \left( 3.385\pm 0.048 \right) \times 10^{-6}$, which after inclusion of charmonium and bottomonium contributions gives a final result $a_τ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO} = \left( 3.501\pm0.048\right) \times 10^{-6}$, comparable to data-driven and lattice predictions. Lattice QCD results along with our value for $a_τ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO}$ are used to obtain a combined QCD determination from lattice and sum-rule methods of $a_μ^\mathrm{HVP,\,LO} = (7.217\pm0.233)\times10^{-8}$. Our analysis provides fundamental QCD sum-rule inputs into the theoretical determinations of $a_τ$, and establishes new methodologies for QCD sum-rule determinations of low-energy observables.
