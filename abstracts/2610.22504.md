# A comparative study of variational quantum classifiers and classical machine learning for $tW-t\bar{t}$ discrimination at the LHC

**Authors:** Rohan Hindocha,  Priyanka

Precise measurements of single top-quark production in the associated $tW$-channel require efficient discrimination against the dominant $t\bar{t}$ background. In this work, we investigate the applicability of quantum machine learning to this classification problem using the Variational Quantum Classifier (VQC) algorithm. Simulated proton-proton collision events from CMS Open Data corresponding to Run-2 are employed to train and evaluate the VQC model. Its performance is compared with two established classical approaches, Boosted Decision Trees (BDTs) and Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) using both machine learning metrics and physics-motivated metrics that describe the Signal Significance as a function of Signal Efficiency. The comparison is performed separately for the dileptonic $e-μ$ channel and for the resolved and boosted hadronic topologies. In addition, signal-enriched and background-enriched phase-space regions are studied to assess the dependence of classifier performance on event topology. Our results provide a systematic benchmark for the application of VQC for a realistic High Energy Physics classification task and identify the analysis regimes wherein quantum-inspired models offer competitive discrimination with respect to established classical techniques.
