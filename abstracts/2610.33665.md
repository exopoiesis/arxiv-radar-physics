# Renewable model averaging for generalized linear models

**Authors:** Yalu Ping, Chen Xuerong, Yu Dalei

Prediction is one of the most important research topics in statistics and machine learning. Moreover, in the domain of streaming data analysis, real-time prediction has become a rapidly growing research field in recent years, owing to the abundance of streaming data. In many fields of scientific research, such as biomedical research, weather forecasting, and traffic management, improving real-time prediction of streaming data is crucial in general. However, the complicated features of streaming data, such as large-scale and high-velocity, impose both theoretical and computational challenges to the development and evaluation of real-time prediction in this field, especially when the investigator has multiple models at hand. To overcome these challenges, we propose a novel Renewable Model Averaging method through Quadratic approximation (ReMAQ) for generalized linear model (GLM) for streaming data. By approximating the model averaging procedure for GLMs (which is typically a convex optimization problem) with a quadratic optimization formulation, ReMAQ allows us to update the model averaging prediction sequentially when a new batch of data arrives, without accessing the entire historical data. Moreover, ReMAQ is also computationally efficient as one only needs to consider a quadratic programming problem in each steps of updating. Under certain regular conditions, we also show that the proposed ReMAQ weight is asymptotically equivalent to that by the oracle model averaging based on the inaccessible full data, the asymptotic optimality of ReMAQ is then established accordingly. The proposed method is also assessed by simulation studies. We apply our proposed ReMAQ in the study of the Airline On-Time Performance Dataset and the results demonstrate the superiority our method in practical situations.
