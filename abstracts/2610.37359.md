# Multifidelity variational inference for derivative-free PDE-constrained inverse problems

**Authors:** Eric J. Parish

We develop a multifidelity black-box variational inference (MF-BBVI) approach for PDE-constrained Bayesian inverse problems in which forward solves are expensive and PDE gradients are unavailable. The method is built on score-function estimators for the evidence lower bound (ELBO), which avoid differentiation through the PDE model but are known to suffer from high variance. To address this high variance, we employ multifidelity approximate control-variate estimators that combine a small number of high-fidelity samples with a larger number of low-fidelity samples to reduce estimator variance and the number of high-fidelity evaluations required for optimization. We further develop a PDE-derivative-free second-order optimization strategy based on both gradient and Hessian score-function estimators. For low-fidelity model generation, we propose an on-the-fly strategy that builds and adaptively updates a reduced-order model (ROM) as the variational distribution evolves throughout the optimization. We investigate strategies for adapting the ROM and estimating the approximate control-variate gains, examining tradeoffs between estimator bias, variance reduction, and computational cost. The resulting approach facilitates derivative-free variational inference for large-scale inverse problems through a reduction in overall computational cost. We demonstrate the method on four numerical examples: a linear benchmark with an exact posterior and three PDE-constrained inverse problems.
