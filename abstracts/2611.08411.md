# Low-Rank Tensor Manifolds for Turbulent Field Reconstruction from Sparse Data

**Authors:** Nazmus Sakib, Alexander G. Mychkovsky, Hessam Babaee

Reconstructing turbulent flow fields from sparse and irregular measurements remains challenging because the underlying inverse problem is severely underdetermined. Here we present MANTRA, a manifold-based tensor framework that represents each velocity component in a continuous tensor-product space while constraining its coefficient tensor to a low-rank Tucker manifold. This structure provides a compact, data-adaptive representation without requiring gridded observations or governing equations. Riemannian optimization and adaptive selection of rank and basis complexity enable the model to exploit coordinate-wise spatiotemporal correlations. In simulations of forced isotropic turbulence and flow over a cylinder, MANTRA recovers multiscale structures at sampling densities orders of magnitude below the underlying grids and remains accurate under Gaussian noise up to 20% of the peak velocity. It also recovers leading coordinate-wise correlation structures from sparse observations. Applied to experimental particle-tracking velocimetry of an oscillating impinging jet, MANTRA reveals time-evolving structures that are not apparent from the scattered measurements alone.
