# A Trajectory-Aware Domain-Decomposed Multispace Reduced Basis Preconditioner for Parametric Helmholtz Problems

**Authors:** An Ping, Zhichao Peng, Pengliang Yang

Repeated solutions of parametric Helmholtz problems arise in multi-query applications such as sensitivity analysis and uncertainty quantification. Classical preconditioned iterative solvers do not automatically reuse low-dimensional structures across parameters. Multispace reduced basis (MSRB) preconditioners can exploit such structures by constructing iteration-specific reduced order models (ROMs) from training data, but storing global iteration-dependent bases incurs substantial memory costs. Building on trajectory-aware MSRB training, we develop a domain-decomposed MSRB (DD-MSRB) preconditioner for parametric Helmholtz systems truncated by perfectly matched layers (PMLs). Within flexible GMRES, each reduced space is trained on inverse-action snapshots along the Krylov trajectories generated by the ROM-based preconditioners applied in preceding iterations. To reduce storage, problem-adapted spatial partitions exploit heterogeneous local snapshot compressibility across the physical interior and the PML. Local basis dimensions in each subdomain are determined greedily under a global snapshot-error budget to balance approximation accuracy and storage. The resulting hybrid solver applies DD-MSRB preconditioning during the initial iterations to capture dominant cross-parameter modes and then switches, when necessary, to a shifted-Laplacian preconditioner to reduce error components poorly represented by the reduced spaces. Numerical experiments on two-dimensional half-space propagation, elliptic-obstacle scattering, and three-dimensional waveguides demonstrate significant acceleration over the shifted-Laplacian baseline and substantial memory savings relative to global MSRB. For the largest waveguide system, with 11.0 million unknowns, DD-MSRB reduces deployed ROM storage by 44.9% and mean online solve time by 30.2% compared with global MSRB.
