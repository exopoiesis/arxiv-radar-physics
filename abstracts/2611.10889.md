# A data-driven approach for systematic correction of electronic bandgaps

**Authors:** Pamela C. Carvalho, Asa Marif, Erna K. Delczeg-Czirjak, Sergei Simak, Utkarsh Singh, Olle Eriksson

We demonstrate that a single machine-learning correction, applied to bandgaps from density functional theory calculations with two of the most frequently used energy functionals, results in bandgaps that are close to the experimental values. This solves a classical problem of computationally efficient electronic structure methods, to give reliable bandgaps that can be compared to experiments. The employed approach relies on materials specific feature engineering of machine learning models, and is shown to have close accuracy to that of much more expensive calculations based on the GW-approximation or hybrid functionals. The findings reported here open the possibility to use efficient calculations in materials design workflows that need reliable bandgaps, with implications for investigations targeting large computational databases or for investigations that are entirely predictive in nature.
