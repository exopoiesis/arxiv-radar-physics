# Reconstruction of pion polarizabilities in lattice QCD with machine learning

**Authors:** Hanxing Jiang, Frank X. Lee

We apply the denoising diffusion probabilistic model (DDPM) from generative machine learning to a model-independent reconstruction of electric and magnetic polarizabilities of both charged and neutral pions. The inputs are the sparse momentum-space four-point-function data in lattice QCD for the connected quark-line contributions obtained in previous simulations at four pion masses between approximately 370 and 1100 MeV on quenched Wilson lattices. For charged pions, the elastic contribution is obtained from the pion form-factor slope, and the complete electric and magnetic inelastic terms are reconstructed separately. The neutral-pion electric and magnetic channels are reconstructed without an elastic charge-radius contribution. Four distinct families of synthetic training functions are employed, so that no single analytic fitting function is prescribed for the lattice data. We present low-momentum reconstructions to the limit of zero momentum transfer where the polarizabilities are defined, and mass extrapolations to the physical pion mass of 138 MeV.
