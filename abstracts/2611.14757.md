# Performance Analysis of Quantization-Aware Neural Channel Estimation with Low-Precision Inference

**Authors:** Joonas Törmälä, SaiKrishna Saketh Yellapragada, Eeli Susan, Esa Ollila

Neural channel estimators can improve radio performance of 5G receivers, but real-time deployment in the radio access network (RAN) imposes strict latency and power budgets. Prior studies of quantized neural channel estimators assess this cost through mean-squared error or analytic operation counts. In this paper, we train a 1D convolutional neural network (CNN) channel estimator for the 5G NR physical uplink shared channel (PUSCH) with INT8 and FP8 quantization-aware training (QAT), and we evaluate its radio performance together with its latency, power, and energy on an NVIDIA H100 GPU. On TDL-A and TDL-C channels at user equipment (UE) speeds up to 35 m/s, both 8-bit models stay within 0.025 dB of FP32 at 10% block error rate (BLER). At batch size 128, INT8 reduces the latency by 16%, the incremental GPU power by 33%, and the energy per query by 26%, compared with 6%, 26%, and 21% for FP8. INT8 is therefore the preferred format.
