# Newton's Method and Policy Iterations for Viscous Hamilton--Jacobi Equations

**Authors:** Diogo A. Gomes, Xiaoqin Guo, Yeoneung Kim, Hung V. Tran, Yuming P. Zhang

We study Newton's method for the Cauchy problem for viscous Hamilton--Jacobi equations with Hamiltonians that need not be convex. Each Newton step solves a linear parabolic equation obtained by linearizing the equation at the current iterate. We prove that the iterates converge geometrically in \(L^\infty\) from any initial guess satisfying the initial condition, and quadratically in the parabolic Hölder space \(C^{2+α,1+α/2}\) from initial guesses close to the solution. When the Hamiltonian admits a control representation, the Newton iterates coincide with those of classical policy iteration, which therefore inherits both convergence rates. We extend the local quadratic convergence to uniformly parabolic fully nonlinear equations. For small viscosity, we introduce a Nash--Moser variant that smooths the iterate and the residual in space, and we give a convergence criterion with constants independent of the viscosity. Finally, we show how computable residuals certify the error of inexact Newton corrections computed with physics-informed neural networks.
