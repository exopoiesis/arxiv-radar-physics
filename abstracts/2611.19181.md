# Scalable and Interpretable Quantum Mixture of Experts for High-Dimensional Spatio-Temporal Data

**Authors:** Junghoon Justin Park, Anna Jung, Kyumin Lee, Samuel Yen-Chi Chen, Huan-Hsin Tseng, Shinjae Yoo, Jiook Cha

Quantum Mixture of Experts (QMoE) architectures offer a promising path toward scalable quantum machine learning, yet existing approaches remain largely confined to low-dimensional benchmarks and often rely on naive input partitioning that disrupts feature dependencies. We address these gaps by introducing Feature-Partitioned QMoE, which partitions high-dimensional spatio-temporal inputs into functionally coherent feature groups and assigns each group to a dedicated quantum expert. We further propose a hierarchical interpretability framework that leverages the modular structure of QMoE to produce circuit-level gating analysis, per-expert gradient saliency, and heterogeneity-based subtype discovery. Applied to ADHD classification from resting-state fMRI (N=4,458, ABCD Study, 180 brain ROIs, 363 timepoints), the proposed Theory-Driven QMoE achieves performance comparable to matched-capacity classical and quantum baselines while enabling circuit-attributable interpretation. The framework identifies FDR-corrected ADHD-associated biomarkers in executive-control and anterior default-mode networks and reveals three neurobiologically meaningful ADHD subtypes. These results position Feature-Partitioned QMoE as a scalable and interpretable methodology for applying quantum machine learning to high-dimensional biomedical data.
