# A Temporal Convolutional Network with Multi-Head Attention Model: A Surrogate for Parallel DEVS Semiconductor Fab Simulation

**Authors:** Vamsi Krishna Pendyala, Hessam S. Sarjoughian, Edward J. Yellig, Bharadhwaj Balasubramaniam

Semiconductor factories process many kinds of wafers organized into lots of various sizes and configurations. Different kinds of machines, each with its own operations, process wafers for hours to days, and the wafers undergo many incremental fabrication steps (with re-entry) and flows spanning days to weeks across connected machines. Fast and accurate predictions of throughput ($TH$) and turnaround time ($TA$) dynamics for individual machines and their combinations are essential for factory operations. To use machine learning methods for this purpose, this paper presents a Temporal Convolutional Network with Multi-head Attention (TCNA) architecture. It uses simulated datasets from a benchmark Parallel DEVS (PDEVS) semiconductor fabrication factory model. The factory processes configurable wafer lots with uniform and sinusoidal wafer arrival profiles and may undergo preventive maintenance. The wafer lot size, arrival profile, and factory maintenance policy are used as static covariates. TCNA is applied to a selected set of simulated discrete-event throughput and turnaround time trajectories. The $TH$ and $TA$ time series predicted by TCNA, evaluated with the common $MAPE$, $MSE$, and $R^2$ measures, show comparable or higher accuracy relative to other model architectures, with similar parameter counts and epoch times; the TCNA model size and execution time are similar to those of other architectures such as the Temporal Fusion Transformer. Although time series predictions are less accurate than high-resolution simulations, they can be deployed at a much lower computational cost. The TCNA achieves orders-of-magnitude speedup over PDEVS simulation with an acceptable loss of accuracy, enabling frequent product scheduling, capacity planning, and maintenance planning.
