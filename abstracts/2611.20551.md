# GranularGPT: Natural-Language-Driven End-to-End Discrete Element Method Simulations

**Authors:** Erlei Li, Sirui Tang, Ai Wang, Mingwei Hu, Yuchen Dai, Geoff Wang

Discrete element method (DEM) simulations are widely used to investigate granular and particulate systems, but their application often requires specialized expertise in particle physics, numerical modelling, and simulation post-processing. Large language models (LLMs) offer new opportunities to simplify these workflows by enabling users to interact with simulation tools through natural language. This study offers GranularGPT, an LLM-assisted agentic framework for end-to-end DEM simulation workflows, from natural-language problem descriptions to simulation execution and automated post-processing. The framework enables users to generate DEM simulation cases, execute and monitor simulations, and automatically extract and report physical and process-level metrics. Evaluation across 60 normal-condition cases covering 12 DEM scenarios and parameter variations achieve a simulation success rate of 91.7%. In the error-handling evaluation, all 10 repairable errors are detected and 9 are successfully resolved, while all 5 non-repairable cases are correctly identified and reported. The framework automatically extracts a range of DEM-relevant metrics, including particle size distributions, packing and density measures, velocity profiles, discharge rates, mixing indices, contact force characteristics, and thermal quantities. GranularGPT provides a foundation for improving the accessibility and efficiency of DEM simulation workflows while reducing the specialized expertise required for their operation.
