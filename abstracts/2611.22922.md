# SWIR-cGAN: Synthesizing SWIR Bands Using VNIR Imagery from CBERS and AMAZONIA-1

**Authors:** Alisson Cleiton de Oliveira, Rennan de Freitas Bezerra Marujo, Thales Sehn Körting

Spectral band synthesis is an important research topic in remote sensing. The Brazilian satellites CBERS-4, CBERS-4A, and AMAZONIA-1 provide high temporal resolution (1-3 days) visible and near-infrared (VNIR) imagery through the Wide-Field Imager (WFI), but lack short-wave infrared (SWIR) bands. Recent deep learning methods have shown promising results for spectral synthesis, but challenges remain for cross-satellite generalization and spectrally distant bands. This paper presents a conditional Generative Adversarial Network (cGAN) with an attention-enhanced U-net generator and a multi-loss objective to synthesize Sentinel-2/MSI SWIR bands 11 (SWIR-1) and 12 (SWIR-2) from WFI VNIR inputs. Experiments used 4,125 patches for training and cross-validation and 1,026 unseen patches for testing, with hyperparameters optimized using a subset of the training data. On SWIR-1, the model achieved SSIM of 0.900, PSNR of 31.44 dB, and MAE of 0.019 in TOA reflectance. On SWIR-2, it achieved SSIM of 0.917, PSNR of 33.35 dB, and MAE of 0.015. Benchmarking was performed on the test dataset, with each model optimized separately prior to evaluation. SWIR-cGAN outperformed the baselines, particularly for SWIR-2. The ablation study identified the multi-loss as the main performance contributor, while the attention and residual modules provided additional improvements. Our results demonstrate the feasibility of synthesizing SWIR bands from WFI imagery. The SWIR-cGAN is available at [https://github.com/marujore/swir-cgan](https://github.com/marujore/swir-cgan).
