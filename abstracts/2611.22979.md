# Residual-Driven Basis and Data Selection for Polynomial Approximation of High-Dimensional Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman Equations

**Authors:** Yuyang Huang, Dante Kalise, Yuhua Zhu

We develop greedy strategies for selecting global polynomial basis functions and training data in the sparse approximation of high-dimensional partial differential equations, with emphasis on Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations arising in nonlinear optimal control. Inspired by the goal-oriented dual-weighted residual framework, we derive a closed-form, mesh-free indicator that predicts, to first order, the reduction of the PDE residual obtained by adding a single candidate basis function, without solving an adjoint problem. In the data-driven regime, we jointly select basis functions and gradient-augmented training samples by maximizing the predicted decrease of a supervised loss. Numerical experiments on benchmark PDEs and nonlinear optimal control problems in up to 35 dimensions show accuracy comparable to non-adaptive approaches with substantially fewer basis functions and training samples.
