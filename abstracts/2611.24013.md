# Topological Quantum Reservoir Computing

**Authors:** Zhi-Lei Zhang, Lianxiang Cui, C. P. Sun, Kohei Nakajima, Kazuyuki Aihara

Scaling universal quantum computation and implementing quantum machine learning with protected logical qubits remain challenging. To harness the computational potential of existing fault-tolerant quantum platforms, we introduce topological quantum reservoir computing, a novel approach using protected degrees of freedom for storage and the host system's intrinsic dynamics for information processing. Using a one-dimensional Kitaev chain, we show how long-lived Majorana edge modes and fast bulk responses support these complementary functions within the same many-body system. We identify the tradeoffs governing access to protected memory and show how matched interfaces enable programmable writing and interactions enable autonomous information consolidation from the bulk to the edge. These mechanisms improve performance on tasks requiring long-term retention of temporal information. Our results establish topology as a new resource for quantum reservoir computing and open a route toward near-term quantum computing and quantum machine learning.
