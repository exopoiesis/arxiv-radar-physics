# LEMUR: A Searchable Web Archive of Deep Chandra Images of Galaxies and Galaxy Clusters

**Authors:** Carter L. Rhea, Julie Hlavacek-Larrondo, Marine Prunier, Hyunseop Choi, Megan Donahue, Christian Thibeault

We present LEMUR, a publicly accessible archive of uniformly reduced, deep Chandra images of 2,569 galaxies and galaxy clusters (including groups), comprising 5,505 ACIS observations and 138.8 Ms of exposure. By combining the publicly available imaging observations of each target, we provide background-subtracted, exposure-corrected images in broad, soft, and hard bands at the native ACIS pixel scale of 0.492 arcsec. Our primary goal is to make the wealth of archival Chandra observations accessible to researchers without requiring specialized expertise in X-ray data reduction.   Each observation is reprocessed with CIAO, filtered for background flares, and reduced with settings chosen to preserve faint diffuse emission; the merged images are exposure corrected and background subtracted using the ACIS blank-sky backgrounds in a broad (0.5-7.0 keV), a soft (0.5-2.0 keV), and a hard (2.0-7.0 keV) band. For every object, we measure an X-ray center at the peak of its diffuse emission, and we provide a morphological similarity search based on embeddings from the AstroCLIP foundation model, in which nearest neighbors share cool-core status in 73% of matched cluster pairs, compared with 51% by chance.   Photometry of 275 point sources agrees with the Chandra Source Catalog to within 3% in the broad band. The images, along with their counts images, background models, exposure maps, and quick-look stamps, are available through a searchable web archive at [https://lemur-archive.lemur-xray.workers.dev/](https://lemur-archive.lemur-xray.workers.dev/). The catalog tables are archived on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24351915), and all code is open source at [https://github.com/crhea93/Lemur](https://github.com/crhea93/Lemur).
