# EuclidFM: Foundation Model for Compact Photometric Survey Representation

**Authors:** Reyhaneh Vojoudi Salmani, Mahsa Berahman, Saeed Ansarifard, Michael M. Fausnaugh, Seyed Mohammad Sadegh Movahed

We present EuclidFM, a compact self-supervised model that learns object-level representations from heterogeneous catalogue photometry in Euclid Quick Data Release 1. Using 90,258 sources across three Euclid Deep Fields, a masked-autoencoder Transformer reconstructs withheld fluxes and uncertainties from available measurements, producing a 128-dimensional embedding per source. The embeddings encode morphological and photometric information: sources with high point-like probability have, on average, 12.4 similarly classified neighbours among their 15 nearest neighbours, compared with 4.3 under field- and magnitude-stratified randomization. Linear models trained on frozen embeddings predict VIS magnitudes with correlations of 0.954-0.980 across three held-out fields. For sources with VIS aperture-flux uncertainties up to 1 microJy, incorporating embeddings reduces the root-mean-square error in predicted log uncertainty by at least 24% relative to a magnitude-only model. A classifier trained on spectroscopically confirmed sources identifies 1,150 quasar candidates, but only 21 of the 156 candidates with accepted external counterparts show AGN evidence, including 12 spectroscopic quasars. The classifier's transferability to the wider photometric catalogue therefore remains uncertain, and its purity and completeness require further evaluation. Independently of classification, EuclidFM provides a common representation for downstream survey analyses when photometric coverage differs between sources. Code and data are available at [https://github.com/AI-Driven-HEP/EuclidFM](https://github.com/AI-Driven-HEP/EuclidFM).
