# Fast and accurate large-volume Lyman-$α$ forest mocks with three-dimensional U-Net trained on IllustrisTNG and FLAMINGO

**Authors:** Bhaskar Arya, Soumak Maitra, Shadab Alam

The Lyman-$α$ forest probes matter structure at $z\gtrsim2$, constraining baryon acoustic oscillations, neutrino masses, and dark matter properties. Interpreting current and upcoming spectroscopic surveys requires mock catalogues spanning Gpc-scale volumes while capturing small-scale intergalactic medium physics. This combination of high resolution and large volume is prohibitively expensive for hydrodynamical simulations. We present a field-level emulator, based on a three-dimensional U-Net, that maps approximate density and velocity fields directly to Ly$α$ optical depth and transmitted flux. At $z\sim3$, we train in small volumes and test on much larger, independent ones in two settings: IllustrisTNG (training on a $110\,\mathrm{cMpc}$ box and testing on a $300\,\mathrm{cMpc}$ box with 2LPT and $N$-body inputs) and FLAMINGO (training on $1\,\mathrm{cGpc}$ and testing on $2.8\,\mathrm{cGpc}$ with $N$-body inputs). The hydrodynamical statistics of TNG and FLAMINGO differ appreciably over their common $k$-range, by $\sim15\%$ in $P_{\rm 1D}$, $\sim17\%$ in the bispectrum, and $\sim40\%$ in the connected trispectrum, although their normalized power-spectrum correlation structures remain broadly similar. Within each suite, the emulator reproduces $P_{\rm 1D}$ to $2.8\%$ in TNG and $5.6\%$ in FLAMINGO, the bispectrum to $4$--$5\%$, and the trispectrum to $10$--$16\%$, while also recovering the flux PDF and broad correlation structure. Field-level emulation thus provides a practical route to large-volume Ly$α$-forest mocks for current and future surveys.
