# Probing Compressed Dark Sectors at a Muon Collider through Dilepton Signatures and Machine Learning

**Authors:** Mohamed Belfkir, Ayoub Hmissou, Larbi Rahili

The inert doublet model (IDM), a minimal extension of the Standard Model (SM), provides a scalar dark matter (DM) candidate that belongs to the additional Higgs doublet. The model faces challenges in achieving the correct relic abundance for compressed spectra and DM masses in the high-mass range. In this work we investigate the prospects of detecting dark matter candidates at future high-energy muon colliders. Focusing on the process $μ^+ μ^- \to AH$ with subsequent decay $A \to H Z^*$ and $Z^* \to \ell^+\ell^-$, we explore the sensitivity of machine learning techniques, specifically Boosted Decision Trees (BDT) and XGBoost, to distinguish the signal from Standard Model backgrounds. We demonstrate that the combination of advanced multivariate analysis with high-luminosity muon collisions enhances the discovery potential for inert scalars in compressed mass spectra scenarios.
