# Observational Aspects of Black Holes in Square-Root Electrodynamic Backgrounds

**Authors:** Maryem Jemri

Motivated by recent thermodynamic investigations \cite{nn}, we study the optical behaviors of black holes in a square-root nonlinear electrodynamic framework. More precisely, we examine the shadow aspect of black holes obtained by considering the electrodynamic Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{F})=\mathcal{F} \sqrt{1-k(2Q^2\mathcal{F})^{1/4}},$ where $\mathcal{F}$ is the Maxwell gauge invariant, $k$ is a relevant dimensionless parameter, and $Q$ is considered as a magnetic charge. By confronting the obtained results with the Event Horizon Telescope collaboration findings, we provide the corroborated moduli space regions. Then, we approach such optical behaviors using machine learning techniques. As a result, we observe a possible compatibility with the Sgr $A^*_{Keck}$ black hole model.
