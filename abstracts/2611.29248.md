# AgenticMM: A Material-Centered Multi-Agent Architecture for Scalable First-Principles Materials Modeling

**Authors:** Zhenkun Yuan, Geoffroy Hautier

Multi-agent frameworks are attracting increasing attention as a means of autonomously driving complex, long-running workflows in first-principles materials modeling. However, such frameworks commonly organize AI agents by computational task or scientific role. When scaled across many materials, this organization can require repeated transfers of material-specific context and complicate persistent execution and resource coordination. Here, we introduce AgenticMM, a material-centered multi-agent architecture in which each material has a persistent agent that owns its complete workflow and retains its scientific context, while a persistent coordinator allocates shared resources, mediates communication, and tracks campaign progress. AgenticMM is designed to incorporate reusable, prompt-based skills as modular definitions of these workflows. Using DefectSkills prompts, we demonstrate the feasibility of AgenticMM through a multistage point-defect campaign in which material-specific agents concurrently perform host electronic-structure, phase-stability, and intrinsic-defect calculations for five chemically diverse semiconductors.
