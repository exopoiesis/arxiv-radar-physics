# Using fixed $w$ to reproduce evolving $w_0$-$w_a$ dark energy at the field level

**Authors:** Yuxi Meng, Daniela Saadeh, Cheng-Zong Ruan, Baojiu Li, Kazuya Koyama

Recent results from the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) hint at dynamical dark energy, making the accurate modelling of such cosmologies increasingly important for future analyses. In this work, we show that because, when written in superconformal time $τ$, the $N$-body equations of motion depend on the background cosmology only through the expansion history $a(τ)$, matching $a(τ)$ allows us to map one cosmology onto another. As a first, observationally relevant, application of this new method, we match a Chevallier--Polarski--Linder (CPL) cosmology to a constant-$w$ cosmology with the closest $a(τ)$. This allows us to use a single fixed-$w$ simulation to reproduce the simulation predictions of the target CPL cosmology at the field level at redshifts $0\le z\le3$. We validate this matching with 100 pairs of CPL and fixed-$w$ \textsc{mg-glam} simulations, spanning $0.1\leΩ_{\rm m}\le0.5$, $-1.4\le w_0\le-0.5$ and $-1.8\le w_a\le-0.15$. For a DESI-like cosmology, the matter power spectra of the matched and target simulations agree to 0.24\% for $k\lesssim1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ and 0.8\% up to $k\lesssim3\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at $0\le z\le3$, and the density phases are reproduced with a stochasticity below 0.02\%. Displacements, velocities, momenta, redshift-space multipoles, bispectra and the halo and void abundances are similarly well predicted. For most models we consider, we achieve subpercent error in the power spectrum up to scales $k\lesssim1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at all redshifts, with density stochasticity below 0.03\% over the same scales. The method allows constant-$w$ simulations and field-level emulators to be used for CPL cosmologies, and generalises to any cosmology with no additional scale dependence.
