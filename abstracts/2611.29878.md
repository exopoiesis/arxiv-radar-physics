# Explicit Response Formulae and Request Problem for Smooth Expanding Endomorphisms

**Authors:** Jianyu Chen, Na Zhang, Yufeng Zhang

In this paper, we investigate the higher order differentiability of physical measures, in the category of smooth expanding endomorphisms of compact manifolds, and we establish the explicit response formulae of any order. The proof is based on the elegant Keller-Liverani-Gouëzel perturbation theory of transfer operators. Furthermore, we provide the solutions to the high order request problem, which is regarded as the inverse problem to response formulae, that is, we make perturbations to realize prescribed responses up to any finite order, and we establish the existence of minimizers for a relaxed optimization problem associated with suitable cost functionals.
