# Identax: A Toolbox for Efficient Learning of Linear, Parameter-Varying, and Nonlinear Systems in JAX

**Authors:** Roel Drenth, Maarten Schoukens, Roland Tóth

Accurate data-driven modelling of dynamical systems is increasingly important across engineering disciplines, yet state-of-the-art identification methods are often computationally demanding, opaque, or poorly scalable. We present Identax, a JAX toolbox for identifying linear, Linear Parameter-Varying (LPV) and nonlinear systems, designed as an extensible foundation for system identification research. Its modular design allows model structures, identification criteria and optimization methods to be extended independently. The first release provides state-space and Linear Fractional Representation (LFR) models, multiple-shooting criteria for parallel training, and a transparent optimization pipeline. On the Silverbox benchmark, an LPV-LFR model outperforms all previous black-box results while training in under three minutes on a CPU. On a control moment gyroscope, Identax matches state-of-the-art accuracy.
