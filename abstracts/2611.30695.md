# Hierarchical Multi-Timescale Deep Reinforcement Learning for QoS-Aware AI-RAN Configuration

**Authors:** Navid Keshtiarast, Amirhassan Babazadeh Darabi, Cem Tarhan, Martin Stahn, Ljiljana Simić, Marina Petrova

Configuring radio access networks (RANs) for heterogeneous services requires coordinating radio parameters whose effects on throughput, delay, and reliability are closely coupled and evolve over different timescales. This paper presents a hierarchical deep reinforcement learning (DRL) framework for joint, QoS-aware RAN configuration that aligns the learning structure with the real-time, near-real-time, and non-real-time control loops of O-RAN that are also adopted by AI-RAN. Three proximal policy optimization agents jointly select per-user modulation and coding schemes, user-to-bandwidth-part (BWP) assignments, per-BWP transmit power, numerology, and BWP bandwidth weights, at three timescales. The agents share a QoS-aware objective combining throughput demand satisfaction with delay and reliability penalties. We extend ns-3 5G-LENA and integrate it with Ray RLlib through ns3-ai to support the required radio controls, network observations, and hierarchical policy execution. We evaluate the agents' performance in a nine-cell downlink network with inter-cell interference, varying user populations, and mixed URLLC, gaming, conversational-video, and video-streaming traffic. Compared with the baseline 5G-NR RAN, our trained hierarchical DRL agents substantially reduce URLLC and conversational-video delays, lower the residual block error rate, and prevent the near-starvation of the weakest flows, trading off slightly lower throughput for high-rate video streaming under capacity-limited conditions.
