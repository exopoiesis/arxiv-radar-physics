# From nested soft-collinear subtraction to NNLO QCD predictions with large language models

**Authors:** Federica Devoto, Kirill Melnikov, Ivan Novikov, Raoul Röntsch, Chiara Signorile-Signorile, Davide Maria Tagliabue, Matteo Tresoldi

We investigate whether large language models can construct reliable and efficient codes for computing NNLO QCD corrections to collider processes, starting from the general formula derived within the nested soft-collinear subtraction scheme. We use Higgs production in weak-boson fusion with and without an additional jet to compare numerical implementations by large language models, subject to various degrees of supervision, against a human-written reference code.
