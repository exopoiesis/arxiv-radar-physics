# Photometric Uncertainty Correction for Time-Domain Surveys: Vera C. Rubin Data Preview 2 Case Study

**Authors:** Konstantin Malanchev, Doug Branton, Sandro Campos, Neven Caplar, Mi Dai, Melissa DeLucchi, Olivia Lynn, Rachel Mandelbaum, Sean McGuire, Inar Timiryasov

Time-domain science, from variability detection to supernova cosmology, relies on accurate photometric uncertainties. We present a data-driven, catalog-level method to validate and improve the photometric uncertainties reported by time-domain surveys. The analysis is based on transforming light curves of non-variable objects into mean-variance whitened signals. We then estimate a correction to the uncertainties by matching the distribution of the whitened signals of all objects to the standard normal distribution, which they ought to follow when the uncertainties are correct. The correction can be modeled as a function of each measurement's flux, reported uncertainty, and available observational metadata, and can be applied to measurements of both non-variable and variable objects. Applied to the Rubin Observatory Data Preview 2 (DP2), the method shows that the uncertainties of bright sources are underestimated by up to a factor of five. The inferred corrections correspond to a magnitude error floor of about 10 mmag for both direct-image and difference-image photometry, decreasing to about 6 mmag in deeper fields. We provide the corrected uncertainties as part of the Rubin DP2 HATS catalogs.
