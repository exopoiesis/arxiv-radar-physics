# Ion and electron temperature recovery for Irkutsk incoherent scatter measurements

**Authors:** V. P. Tashlykov, A. V. Medvedev, V. P. Lebedev, I. V. Kichigin

Recovering ion and electron plasma temperatures from Irkutsk Incoherent Scatter Radar (IISR) data is a challenge due to rapid Faraday rotation, which significantly distorts signal correlation profiles. This paper presents an adaptation of the full-profile analysis (FPA) method tailored to the specific conditions of the IISR using a two-step inverse problem framework. The core novelty of the approach lies in the integration of data from both narrowband and wideband channels: experimental power profiles from the wideband channel are incorporated directly into the forward model for temperature recovery. By mathematically establishing the link between the wideband signal amplitude and the temperature ratio, we introduce a non-linear constraint that significantly narrows the solution search space. This addresses the problem of "stretched" global minima in the objective functional, effectively filtering out physically inconsistent results. The inverse problem is solved using the Improved Stochastic Ranking Evolution Strategy (ISRES), which enables fully automated parameter estimation without the need for manual initial guesses.
