PINNs for N=2* holography

Alex Buchel

arXiv:2610.14461·hep-th·Published 2026-10-10

Can a holographic theory be reconstructed from its equation of state? We investigate this question using physics-informed neural networks in the bosonic-mass sector of the Pilch--Warner model. We first validate the PINN approach against an analytic solution encoding the leading high-temperature corrections to the conformal equation of state. We then reconstruct nonlinear black-brane geometries and the scalar potential using thermodynamics, ultraviolet asymptotics, Ward identities, and horizon regularity. Independent solutions of the bulk Einstein-scalar equations demonstrate that accurately reproducing thermodynamics with a trained PINN does not guarantee an accurate reconstruction of the scalar potential. High-precision knowledge of the high-temperature equation of state constrains nonlinear interactions in the bulk scalar potential. Fixing the cubic coupling to its exact PW value, motivated by this high-temperature extraction, reduces the maximum sampled error in the reconstructed scalar self-interactions by a factor of $\sim 15$ compared with constraining only the bulk scalar mass. Adding knowledge of the bulk viscosity of the gauge theory plasma further reduces this error by an additional factor of $\sim 13$, with independent forward solutions reproducing the bulk viscosity within $0.09\%$.
