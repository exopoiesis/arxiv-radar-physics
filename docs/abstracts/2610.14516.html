UAV SAR Imaging via Diffusion Posterior Sampling under Non-Ideal Motion in ISAC Systems

Keying Zhu, Le Liang, Jie Yang, Fan Liu, Shi Jin

arXiv:2610.14516·eess.SP·Published 2026-10-10

Unmanned aerial vehicle-enabled synthetic aperture radar (SAR) offers significant potential for providing communication services to ground users while sensing the surrounding environment in low-altitude integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems. However, its practical deployment is challenged by known non-ULM trajectories and unknown position errors, which violate conventional SAR assumptions and cause severe image defocusing. We address these non-ideal impacts by formulating the ISAC SAR imaging as an exact linear inverse problem to accurately model arbitrary trajectories with multiple-input multiple-output orthogonal frequency division multiplexing waveforms. Based on this formulation, we develop a diffusion posterior sampling (DPS)-based super-resolution SAR framework, where a pre-trained diffusion model provides a data-driven image prior and the exact physical model enforces measurement consistency during posterior inference. The inference process is further accelerated for practical implementation. For unknown position errors, we develop a joint iterative DPS and motion compensation (MoCo) optimization algorithm with discrete cosine transform reparameterization and coarse-to-fine spectral scheduling. Simulations demonstrate that our framework achieves high-accuracy reconstruction across diverse trajectories and remarkable super-resolution, maintaining greater than 21.3 dB peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) even under half physical resolution truncation. Under unknown motion errors, the joint DPS and MoCo algorithm achieves 23.65 dB PSNR with a residual position-error estimation error of 0.19 cm.
