Brownian GPDs: A Stochastic Calculus Approach to Generalized Parton Distributions from Integral Constraints

Douglas Q. Adams, Simonetta Liuti, Zaki Panjsheeri, Brannon Semp

arXiv:2610.20458·hep-ph·Published 2026-10-14

We present a stochastic calculus approach for modeling generalized parton distribution functions using information provided from lattice QCD calculations of Mellin moments, without assuming a functional form. Reconstructing the underlying dependence on the partonic momentum fraction from a limited number of moments constitutes an intrinsically non-unique inverse problem. Our goal is to provide a model agnostic way to determine GPDs subject to integral constraints. Thus, we derive and show usage of a controlled Langevin approach based on stochastic calculus and Brownian motion. The method combines a variational formulation, based on minimizing a Dirichlet action subject to the imposed constraints, with stochastic dynamics that generates an ensemble of admissible functions. The resulting ensemble provides a preferred reconstruction and characterizes the variability associated with incomplete integral information within the chosen stochastic model. The framework can accommodate general integral kernels and prescribed function values, offering a flexible approach to reconstructing functions from indirect constraints. Beyond its application to GPD phenomenology, the method provides a general strategy for investigating inverse problems in which integral observables are known but the underlying functional dependence remains undetermined. The approach may have more general applications beyond the scope of studying 3D hadron structure, and it is particularly useful in situations where we know definite integrals of functions with kernels, but do not know the underlying function.
