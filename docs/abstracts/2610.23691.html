Reconstruction of Magnetic Fields with a Physics-Informed Neural Network

P. Adhikari, R. Bhattarai, J. Brewington, D. Buskirk, F. B. Hills, Md F. Hossain, R. McDonald, P. A. Palamure, A. Reid, S. Sharmin, A. Tewsley-Booth, B. Plaster

arXiv:2610.23691·physics.ins-det·Published 2026-10-16

Accurate reconstruction of magnetic fields and their gradients is essential for assessing and controlling systematic effects in precision measurement experiments. We have developed a physics-informed neural network (PINN) method which reconstructs three-dimensional magnetic fields subject to the source-free Maxwell equations, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}=0$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{0}$. In an extension of our previous work on PINN methods for reconstruction of magnetic fields in inaccessible interior regions via measurements at exterior locations, we now demonstrate the feasibility of applying PINN methods to the extraction of magnetic field gradient terms from discrete measurement points subject to constraints from Maxwell's equations.
