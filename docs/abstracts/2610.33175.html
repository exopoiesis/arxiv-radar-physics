A Gaussian Process State Space Formulation for Materials Constitutive Modeling: Application to Quasi-brittle Materials

Houlin Xu, Chenyang Li, K. T. Ramesh, Michael D. Shields

arXiv:2610.33175·cs.CE·Published 2026-10-23

Constitutive models for history-dependent, path-dependent materials typically encode this dependence in prescribed functional forms through internal variables such as damage or plastic strain that are phenomenological, model-dependent, and not directly observable. These rigid forms lack flexibility to match observations, make calibration potentially non-unique, and complicate uncertainty quantification. This paper develops a Gaussian-process state-space model (GP-SSM) that replaces the fixed form of the constitutive model with a flexible GP regression, and the prescribed internal variables with latent state variables learned directly from observable stress-strain histories. As a GP, the model is naturally endowed with a measure of uncertainty and can be constrained to satisfy physical principles. We demonstrate this framework for quasi-brittle materials under triaxial compression, where the latent variable evolution is driven by the current-state stress invariants and the increment of equivalent strain to reflect irreversible, pressure-sensitive history. A sparse multi-output GP then maps the latent state and current strain tensor to the six-component stress response, trained by variational sparse inference. The trained model is exported into Abaqus/Explicit through a user material subroutine. Four verification cases are reported: (1) offline rollout on validation and test histories; (2) a single element under changing confinement; (3) a frozen-memory unloading-reloading extension; and (4) a multi-element structural model compared against the Karagozian and Case Concrete (KCC) model. These results show that the learned state-space update reproduces held-out RVE-scale histories in closed-loop rollout, tracks evolving confinement, supports unloading without violating the irreversibility of the learned history state, and reproduces structural-level behavior consistent with KCC.
