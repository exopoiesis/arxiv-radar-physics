End-to-end Particle Reconstruction with Transformers in High-Granularity Calorimeters

Qiming Bao, Haotian Bi, Benedikt Maier, Alex Tapper, Lauri Laatu

arXiv:2610.37798·physics.ins-det·Published 2026-10-27

The High-Luminosity upgrade of the Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC) will drastically increase the number of simultaneously interacting particles, posing a severe challenge to current particle reconstruction methods. While next-generation highly granular calorimeters offer unprecedented 3D measurement capabilities, reconstructing particles from heavily overlapping energy showers remains computationally difficult. This study investigates how raw detector hits can be accurately reconstructed using end-to-end transformer based methods on a simulated calorimeter dataset. We evaluate and compare three distinct machine learning frameworks, focusing on two generative architectures: diffusion and flow matching as well as query-based set prediction. Our results demonstrate that these advanced methods provide highly effective and scalable alternatives for resolving complex overlapping particle showers in conditions encountered in future calorimeters.
