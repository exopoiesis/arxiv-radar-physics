Neural Stream Function Method for Electrical Impedance Tomography with Lipschitz Conductivities

Xiaosheng Li

arXiv:2610.38064·math.NA·Published 2026-10-27

Electrical impedance tomography (EIT) reconstructs the conductivity inside an object from voltage and current measurements on its boundary. The resulting inverse problem is nonlinear and severely ill-posed. Neural networks offer a flexible computational approach, but training physics informed neural networks for EIT and relating their training loss to reconstruction accuracy remain challenging. We develop a novel constrained neural stream function method for planar EIT with Lipschitz conductivities. The method builds the conductivity constraints and prescribed boundary voltages into the neural trial functions and replaces the second order conductivity equation with a first order system in strong form. The measured current is also used to construct a fixed baseline for the stream function. For this formulation, we derive error estimates that relate the sampled neural objective to errors in the physical boundary currents and the reconstructed conductivity. The error estimates separate the contributions from neural approximation, measurement noise, and sampling. Numerical experiments further demonstrate that the proposed method achieves high reconstruction accuracy with both exact and noisy boundary data.
