GeoSim: Benchmarking Multi-Field Neural Operators for Geodynamics

Hailong Liu, Dinghui Yang, Mengxue Liu, Rui Qi, Meng Zhao, Yihang Tang, Jin Wen, Zhilong Fang

arXiv:2611.04517·physics.geo-ph·Published 2026-11-03

Learning reliable surrogates for geodynamic models requires benchmarks that resolve coupled fields and temporal stability. We present GeoSim, a benchmark of high-resolution two-dimensional mantle-plume simulations generated with I2VIS, comprising 237 trajectories across two parameter regimes, with six coupled fields, physical timestamps, and trajectory-level splits. GeoSim defines short-window prediction and autonomous rollout, and evaluates representative neural operators field by field. Across models, we observe a pronounced scalar--velocity gap: scalar fields are reconstructed accurately while velocity errors remain large, and short-window accuracy does not imply stable rollout. We also evaluate MFGNO (Multi-Field Graph Neural Operator), a joint multi-field graph baseline designed for this benchmark; on GeoSim-Plume1, under a fixed per-field objective, it reduces horizontal and vertical velocity errors by 58.4% and 38.5% relative to an independent-field graph baseline, while an explicit field-difference input shows no measurable benefit at this scale. GeoSim provides a reproducible benchmark for field-wise diagnosis and long-horizon evaluation of geodynamic surrogates.
