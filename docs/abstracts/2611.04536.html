Machine learning emulators of dynamic global vegetation models for fast gridded carbon budget predictions

Piyu Ke, Xiaofan Gui, Stephen Sitch, Zhu Liu, Xun Li, Jiang Bian, Pierre Friedlingstein, Philippe Ciais

arXiv:2611.04536·physics.ao-ph·Published 2026-11-03

The land carbon sink explains most of the variability of the atmospheric CO2 growth rate, but the Global Carbon Budget estimates it with dynamic global vegetation models (DGVMs) run once a year. Emulators could provide it sooner if they reproduce each model outside its training period. Here we show that convolutional neural network emulators of 19 of these models, driven by ERA5 climate and their own past predictions, reproduce the global land sink of these models and its spread over two years not used in training. Global monthly net biome production (NBP) anomalies were reproduced with a median R^2 of 0.81. The error of the multi-model mean annual NBP was at most 0.23 GtC yr^-1. At the grid-cell level, the median R^2 of monthly anomalies was 0.30, partly because of errors that accumulate in the emulated NBP history.
