MP-DualNet: A Dual-Branch Deep Learning Approach for Magnetopause Boundary Detection Using Simulated SXI Images

Cheng Zhong, Zhongwei Yang, Tianran Sun, Chengyan Huang, Jiajia Liu, Jingnan Guo, Yuming Wang, Chi Wang

arXiv:2611.07433·astro-ph.SR·Published 2026-11-05

The Earth's magnetopause is a key boundary governing solar wind-magnetosphere coupling. The Solar wind Magnetosphere Ionosphere Link Explorer (SMILE) mission will provide global soft X-ray imaging of the magnetosphere through solar wind charge exchange (SWCX) emission, offering a new opportunity for remote sensing of magnetopause dynamics. However, the limited field of view (FOV) and variable observation geometry of the Soft X-ray Imager (SXI) pose challenges for reliable magnetopause detection. In this study, we develop MP-DualNet, an end-to-end dual-branch deep learning framework for automated magnetopause detection from simulated SXI observations. The framework jointly performs coarse localization to determine magnetopause visibility and relative position within the FOV, and fine-scale boundary localization through pixel-level segmentation. The model is trained and evaluated using line-of-sight magnetopause envelope maps derived from global hybrid simulations under different solar wind and interplanetary magnetic field conditions. The results demonstrate stable and consistent performance, with a classification accuracy exceeding 0.94 and a segmentation IoU above 0.97. For the test set, the mean absolute angular displacement from the reference boundary was 0.488 degrees. The model maintains robust performance across a wide range of noise levels and generalizes well, as demonstrated by its application to an independent storm event. These results indicate that MP-DualNet provides a reliable and generalizable approach for magnetopause detection in SXI observations, supporting future 3D reconstruction and space weather studies in the context of the SMILE mission.
