DrivAerRim: A large-scale full-vehicle CFD dataset for wheel rim aerodynamics

Chao Xia, Xi Zeng, Alexey Vdovin, Huanxia Wei, Simone Sebben, Carl Lewerth, Qing Jia, Zhigang Yang

arXiv:2611.08842·cs.CE·Published 2026-11-06

The wheel region contributes substantially to vehicle aerodynamic drag, and rim geometry strongly influences the flow in this region. However, publicly available automotive aerodynamics datasets offer limited variation in rim design. We present DrivAerRim, a large-scale dataset of full-vehicle computational fluid dynamics simulations covering 904 DeepWheel rim designs with diverse spoke layouts, opening areas and topologies. All cases use the same DrivAer Estateback, operating conditions and numerical setup, with rim geometry as the sole design variable. Wheel rotation is modelled using moving reference frame regions around the rims and rotating wall conditions on the deformed tyres. The numerical setup is assessed through mesh sensitivity analysis and comparisons with wind tunnel measurements. The resulting 9.32 TB release includes wheel geometries, vehicle and component force coefficients, accumulated drag, surface pressure, wall shear stress, and flow fields around the vehicle and wheels. Together, these data support surrogate model training and evaluation across diverse rim geometries, as well as aerodynamic optimisation and generative design to accelerate wheel rim development for vehicle drag reduction.
