FoundaRF: A Physics-Informed Foundation Model Framework for Radio-Frequency Passive Components

Houbo He, Chris Han, Hyunsu Chae, Lei Xia, Qiang Zhou, Lawrence Liu, Sensen Li, David Z. Pan, Taiyun Chi

arXiv:2611.09003·eess.SP·Published 2026-11-06

Designing RF/mmWave passive components is a time-consuming process that relies heavily on expert knowledge and iterative EM simulations. Although recent ML surrogate models have shown great promise in accelerating passive design, they remain task-specific and lack generalization. This paper presents FoundaRF, a foundation model framework that unifies passive layout modeling across diverse layout structures and technologies. It combines a self-supervised encoder with adaptive layout processing and physics-aware decoders to enable few-shot transfer. Experiments across four semiconductor technologies show strong generalization to unseen structures, outperforming baselines with up to a 3.3x reduction in prediction error, reducing labeled-data requirements by up to fourfold, and substantially shortening design turnaround. FoundaRF establishes a scalable and data-efficient solution for RF/mmWave passive design automation.
