Kinematic Reconstruction of Top-Quark Pairs Using Analytical and Machine-Learning Methods with CMS Open Data

V. Aushev, M. Spitsyn, R. Yermolenko, O. Zenaiev

arXiv:2611.09797·hep-ex·Published 2026-11-06

The reconstruction of the top-quark-pair ($t\bar{t}$) kinematics in the dilepton $t\bar{t}$ decay channel is challenging due to the presence of two undetected neutrinos. In this work, existing kinematic reconstruction methods used by the CMS Collaboration are studied using a Monte Carlo sample with $t\bar{t}$ events from CMS Open Data at a centre-of-mass energy of 7 TeV. An improved version of the loose kinematic reconstruction, LKRv2, is developed by imposing more stringent requirements on the jet-lepton pairing entering the reconstruction. A further modification, LKRv3, exploits the kinematic similarity between the dilepton and neutrino-pair systems and improves the resolution of the reconstructed $t\bar{t}$ invariant mass relative to the baseline LKR by $7\%$, with smaller improvements for the transverse momentum and rapidity of $t\bar{t}$. In addition, two machine-learning (ML) based approaches are developed using a residual multilayer perceptron and a conditional normalizing flow. Relative to LKRv3, they improve the resolution of the invariant mass, transverse momentum and rapidity by approximately $30\%$, $20\%$ and $15\%$, respectively. In particular, a resolution of 30 GeV is achieved for the reconstructed invariant mass of $t\bar{t}$ near the production threshold. A dedicated generator-level study and a generator-to-detector transfer test demonstrate that the ML approaches learn a genuine physics-driven correction to the $ν\barν$ approximation, rather than merely exploiting detector-specific effects. These results demonstrate the strong potential of ML-based approaches for improving the reconstruction of dilepton $t\bar{t}$ events, while identifying the neutrino-pair approximation as an important limitation on the achievable resolution.
