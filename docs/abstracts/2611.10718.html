FedSGN: Federated Sparse Grassmann Newton Learning for IoT Anomaly Detection

Jingjing Liu, Xiansen Ju, Xianchao Xiu, Wanquan Liu

arXiv:2611.10718·cs.LG·Published 2026-11-07

Anomaly detection plays a critical role in securing the Internet of things (IoT). Although existing federated principal component analysis (PCA) methods can learn the subspace of normal samples without sharing raw data, they often suffer from feature redundancy that may degrade detection performance. To address this limitation, we propose a novel and effective federated sparse Grassmann Newton (FedSGN) learning method for IoT anomaly detection. Technically, our FedSGN leverages the $\ell_{2,0}$-norm constraint on the global projection matrix to eliminate redundancy and introduces an orthogonality penalty to preserve the intrinsic structure. Moreover, we develop an augmented Lagrangian solver integrated with a semismooth Newton scheme to enable parallel client updates and Grassmann projection for robustness. Numerical experiments demonstrate that the proposed method achieves favorable overall detection performance while reducing communication overhead. The source code is publicly available at https://github.com/xianchaoxiu/FedSGN
