Bridging Gaps with Tensor Networks: From the Haldane Gap and Beyond

Sirui Lu

arXiv:2611.11049·quant-ph·Published 2026-11-08

Proofs generated by large language models are often difficult to understand, generalize, or formalize directly. Here we verify, digest, and formalize OpenAI's computer-assisted proof of a uniform gap in the periodic spin-1 Heisenberg chain using tensor-network diagrams and Lean 4. Reading the partition-function network along space and imaginary time shows how finite-ring bounds propagate across doubled lengths and inverse temperatures to all longer even rings, sharpening the spin-1 bound from the same finite inputs by a factor of 19 to $Δ_L > 0.0729$ for all even $L \geq 36$. We identify reflection positivity as the general condition behind the proof and certify uniform gaps in seven additional spin chains and ladders. More broadly, digesting machine-generated proofs into transparent physical mechanisms yields sharper bounds, broader theorems, and reusable formal verifications.
