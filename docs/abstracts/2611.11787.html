Structure-Informed Electromagnetic Feedback Control of the Vlasov-Maxwell System with Delayed Observations

Jingcheng Lu, Li Wang

arXiv:2611.11787·math.NA·Published 2026-11-09

Feedback control of kinetic plasma instabilities is challenging because observing the full phase-space distribution is costly, while sensing and actuation inevitably introduce delay. We develop a structure-informed electromagnetic feedback framework for the Vlasov-Maxwell system that uses only macroscopic observations to control the full kinetic distribution. We first show that magnetic actuation alone does not directly regulate radial velocity moments, motivating the use of combined electric and magnetic controls. A nonlinear perturbation estimate yields an instantaneous cancellation-dissipation feedback law. To account for delayed observations, we then use Maxwell's equations to identify the macroscopic quantities governing the resulting cancellation errors, namely currents and spatial field gradients. These quantities serve as inputs to a low-rank neural operator that learns a residual correction to the delayed cancellation baseline. Thus, while the feedback depends only on low-dimensional macroscopic observables, the control objective remains the stabilization of the full distribution toward a prescribed kinetic equilibrium. Numerical experiments for two-stream and Weibel-type instabilities demonstrate effective long-time stabilization under substantial feedback delays.
