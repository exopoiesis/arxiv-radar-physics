Joint Bayesian Inference of Dense Matter and f(Q) Gravity with Nuclear and Neutron Star Constraints

N. K. Patra, Sneha Pradhan, P. K. Sahoo, Kai Zhou

arXiv:2611.14553·gr-qc·Published 2026-11-03

We investigate neutron star (NS) properties in symmetric teleparallel $f(Q)$ gravity while consistently accounting for uncertainties in the dense matter equation of state (EOS). We focus on the linear $f(Q)$ model and perform a joint Bayesian inference of the nuclear matter and modified gravity parameters using a Taylor expansion EOS around nuclear saturation density. The analysis incorporates complementary constraints from nuclear saturation properties, chiral effective field theory calculations of pure neutron matter, heavy-ion collision, and empirical nuclear data, the GW170817 tidal deformability posterior, and NICER mass--radius measurements. The resulting posterior distributions simultaneously constrain the dense matter and gravity sectors and quantify their correlations within a common framework. For a canonical $1.4\,M_\odot$ NS, we obtain $R_{1.4}=11.28^{+0.35}_{-0.31}\,\mathrm{km}$ and $Λ_{1.4}=151.4^{+44.9}_{-31.2}$, while the maximum mass is constrained to $M_{\rm max}=2.22^{+0.29}_{-0.16}\,M_\odot$. The modified gravity parameters exhibit only weak direct correlations with most stellar observables, while a pronounced degeneracy remains within the gravity sector. Our results show that accounting for EOS uncertainties is essential for quantifying the interplay between dense matter physics and modified gravity and provide a systematic framework for confronting symmetric teleparallel gravity with nuclear and multimessenger NS observations.
