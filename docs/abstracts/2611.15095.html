Data-driven non-contiguous photon-energy groups for fast multigroup radiation transport in line-dominated plasmas

Shinsuke Fujioka, Yuji Takagi, Nozomi Tanaka, Akira Sasaki, Yu Yamamoto, Muhammad Fauzan Syahbana, Katsunobu Nishihara, Tomoyuki Johzaki, Atsushi Sunahara, Masashi Yoshimura

arXiv:2611.15095·physics.plasm-ph·Published 2026-11-11·Updated 2026-11-14

Radiation transport in line-dominated plasmas, such as extreme ultraviolet (EUV) light source plasma, laser-fusion hohlraums, and stellar interiors, needs too many photon-energy groups for accurate radiation-hydrodynamic simulations. Our data-driven method assigns the bins of an opacity table to $K$ non-contiguous groups with fixed spectral shapes that never couple. Existing multigroup codes can use it by replacing the opacity table.In a radiation-hydrodynamic code, 30 such groups replaced 151 conventional groups; the energy conversion efficiency from laser to EUV changed by less than 0.5\% in absolute value.
