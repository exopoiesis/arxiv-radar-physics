Fast forward-and-backward creation of non-equilibrium Gibbs state in driven dissipative quantum systems

Katsuhiro Nakamura, Otabek Normukhamedov, Iwan Setiawan, Jasur Matrasulov

arXiv:2611.17738·quant-ph·Published 2026-11-13

We present the fast-forward (FF) approach to adiabatic time evolution of an open dissipative quantum system within the framework of the Gorini-Kosaakowski-Sudarshan-Lindblad (GKSL) equation. Taking a harmonic oscillator (HO) and a quantum spin as prototype systems under a rapid change of the driving field and in contact with the heat reservoir, we reveal the protocol which creates the non-equilibrium Gibbs state. After preparing the Gibbs state under the fixed external field in advance, we begin to change adiabatically the field. To keep a quasi-adiabatic version of the Gibbs state as the solution of GKSL equation, we solve the inverse problem to obtain the non-adiabatic corrections both to Hamiltonian and to the dissipator. Then we apply the fast forward-and-backward (abbreviated as FF for convenience) time-rescaling, obtaining the FF variants of both Hamiltonian and detailed balance condition (DBC) among dissipative coefficients. The present scheme generates the time-periodic Gibbs state as if a limit-cycle solution of the innovated GKSL equation under the time-periodic driving field. Thanks to the FF variant of DBC, we see the energy conservation of power and heat current of the system, and reversibility of whole system including a reservoir. The requirement of positivity of the FF dissipative coefficients imposes a constraint on the speed of the FF driving. At low temperature, the upper speed limit of the FF periodic driving is found to be proportional to $\tildeβ^{-1/2}\exp(a\tildeβ)$ with $\tildeβ$ the scaled inverse temperature and $a$ the positive constant of $O(1)$.
