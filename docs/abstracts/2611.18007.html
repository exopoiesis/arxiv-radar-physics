Emulating spatially extended systems using Fourier Neural Operator

Gabriele Poccianti, Duccio Fanelli

arXiv:2611.18007·cond-mat.dis-nn·Published 2026-11-13

Operator Learning (OL) algorithms approximate underlying mathematical operators by mapping entire classes of input functions to their corresponding solutions. For instance, architectures such as the Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) have been trained on numerical datasets generated by isolated, stiff ionic models to provide a data-driven alternative to traditional numerical integration schemes. However, real-world biological and physical systems typically consist of interconnected spatial units arranged in complex, heterogeneous networks. Motivated by this observation, we generalize this data-driven framework to spatially extended systems defined on diverse graph topologies, ranging from Erdos-Renyi to Barabasi-Albert networks. Furthermore, we evaluate the proposed approach on empirical data, demonstrating its ability to accurately and efficiently model task-oriented neural activity.
