Sample-Efficient Control of Magnetoconvective Flows via Differentiable Simulation

Jannis Becktepe, Dmitry Krasnov, Nils Thuerey, Jörg Schumacher, Sebastian Peitz

arXiv:2611.18008·cs.LG·Published 2026-11-13

Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) fluid flows are central to demanding engineering applications, most prominently fusion blanket cooling, where flow control is a promising tool to optimize key properties such as heat transfer. While active flow control (AFC) has recently advanced through machine learning, its application to MHD flows remains largely unexplored. In this work, we present $φ$-PICT, a novel differentiable GPU-accelerated MHD solver targeted at AFC. We validate $φ$-PICT against MHD benchmarks and demonstrate its high computational efficiency. Integrated into FluidGym, $φ$-PICT enables sample-efficient gradient-based algorithms to improve heat transfer by $>25$% and allows optimizers to achieve $10^3\times$ higher accuracy in inverse design than black-box methods. $φ$-PICT is publicly available at https://github.com/upb-sciml/phiPICT.
