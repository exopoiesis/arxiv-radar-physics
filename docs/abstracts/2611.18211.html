Cubic Galileon Gravity on Nonlinear Scales with \texttt{Hi-COLA} for LSST Year 1

C. M. A. Zanoletti, A. Sen Gupta, N. Ramachandra, T. Baker, B. Fiorini, K. Heitmann, D. Jones, C. D. Leonard, B. Li, N. Šarčević, for the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration

arXiv:2611.18211·astro-ph.CO·Published 2026-11-13

Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST) is in a prime position to test Modified Gravity (MG) with Weak Lensing (WL) and Large Scale Structure (LSS). However, many nonlinear MG emulators in the literature enforce a $Λ$CDM background by design, missing where data suggest the most interesting deviations lie. We present an emulator for the 3D nonlinear matter power spectrum in Cubic Galileon gravity, a Horndeski theory with a single additional MG parameter, $f_φ$, that modifies the background, growth, and lensing. Trained on \texttt{Hi-COLA} simulations with Gaussian Process Regression (GPR), the emulator achieves $\lesssim 2\%$ accuracy for $k \leq 1.2\ h\,\text{Mpc}^{-1}$ at the fiducial cosmology relative to \texttt{Hi-COLA}, and $\lesssim 5\%$ relative to full N-body \texttt{EFT-RAMSES} simulations. Additionally, conservative scale cuts excluding angular bins affected by baryonic physics enable unbiased parameter estimation in LSST Year 1 weak lensing and galaxy clustering ($3\times2$pt) forecasts, even when validated against simulated data based on higher-resolution N-body simulations. This demonstrates that, within a simplified LSST Y1 setup and the adopted scale cuts, \texttt{Hi-COLA}-based emulation is sufficient to develop inference infrastructure with Stage IV precision for Horndeski gravity models previously unmodeled on nonlinear scales.
