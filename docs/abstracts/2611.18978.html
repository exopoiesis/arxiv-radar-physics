Bayesian Learning of Uncertain Linear System Behaviours

Noreen Abdelwahab, Alberto Padoan, Dominic Liao-McPherson

arXiv:2611.18978·eess.SY·Published 2026-11-13

We present a Bayesian approach to learning uncertain behavioural representations of linear dynamical systems from noisy input-output data. In behavioural systems theory, the finite-horizon behaviour of a linear system is represented by a subspace. We quantify uncertainty in this subspace through probability distributions on the Grassmann manifold. This leads to a geometric formulation of Bayesian inference over the Grassmannian. We derive the posterior distribution under Gaussian measurement noise and discuss how uncertainty in the inferred behaviour can be propagated to trajectory predictions, with a view towards data-driven control.
