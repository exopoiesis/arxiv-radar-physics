Learning inadequacy of Bayesian neural operators for PDE-constrained inference and design

Pratyush Kumar Singh, Lianghao Cao, E. Bruce Pitman, Danial Faghihi

arXiv:2611.19115·cs.AI·Published 2026-11-14

Partial differential equations (PDEs) underpin predictive modeling across science, engineering and medicine. Their use in digital twins, parameter inference and optimal design requires repeated solutions across parameters and scenarios. For nonlinear, transient and multiphysics systems, these calculations can make numerical simulation prohibitively expensive. Here we introduce the residual-informed Bayesian neural operator (RI-BNO), which learns operator inadequacy using a posteriori error estimates from the governing PDEs and incorporates its correction and uncertainty into Bayesian training and prediction. We establish local error bounds for the residual-informed estimates under stability and smoothness assumptions. We demonstrate RI-BNO on a non-linear reaction-diffusion benchmark with DeepONet and latent-subspace architectures, Markov chain Monte Carlo inference of glioma parameter fields from magnetic resonance imaging, and Monte Carlo risk-averse optimization of three-dimensional thermal breaks in building envelopes under manufacturing uncertainty. Across the evaluated examples, RI-BNO improves agreement with finite element (FE) reference predictions, parameter posteriors and optimized designs while using half as many full nonlinear or transient training simulations as the vanilla Bayesian neural operator (vBNO). For thermal break design, online optimization time decreases from 178 hours with the FE model to 18 minutes with RI-BNO. These results demonstrate the value of explicit inadequacy modeling in neural operators for reliable PDE-based inference and design.
