From Non-parametric Statistics to Images: Generating Galaxy Morphologies

Quanfeng Xu, Mi Chen, Renhao Ye, Siyuan Chen, Nan Li, Shiyin Shen

arXiv:2611.19211·astro-ph.GA·Published 2026-11-14

Non-parametric statistics characterize complex galaxy morphologies but do not specify how to construct images that reproduce those statistics. We present Flow-VAE, which combines a variational autoencoder with a conditional normalizing flow to generate images conditioned on concentration, asymmetry, smoothness (CAS), Gini coefficient (G), and M20. A Gaussian mixture model completes unspecified descriptors and measures the training-distribution support of requested combinations. Using a dataset of 240,000 DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys r-band images, we evaluate fidelity by remeasuring morphological statistics from model outputs and comparing them with the inputs. With a tolerance of +/-10% of each descriptor's adopted range, descriptor-specific recovery rates are 96.62-99.97% for reconstruction and 87.81-99.83% for single-parameter generation among valid measurements. Generated descriptors show regression toward typical training values, while less-supported joint conditions produce broader residuals and systematic offsets. These results quantify the extent to which Flow-VAE can generate galaxy images consistent with prescribed non-parametric morphological statistics.
