Read Before You Fit: Text-Derived Constraints for Discovering Differential Equations from Noisy Biological Data

Pronoma Banerjee, Md. Masudur Rahman, Juan P. Wachs

arXiv:2611.19779·cs.AI·Published 2026-11-15

Large language models are increasingly used for scientific equation discovery. However, most existing methods are strongly data-driven and assume sufficiently clean and representative observations. This assumption is problematic in biological systems, where individual-level measurements are often sparse, noisy, heterogeneous, and expensive to obtain. While the underlying mechanisms are extensively described in scientific text, they are not explicitly used for the LLM based modeling of such systems. We introduce P2C, an agentic framework that translates textual descriptions of biological systems into a typed set of formal constraints, and use these constraints to restrict candidate ordinary differential equation (ODE) structures before numerical fitting. P2C consists of five stages: Text2Constraint extraction, ODE discovery, symbolic verification, parameter fitting, and model scoring. We evaluate P2C on four published ODE systems of acute inflammation and immune regulation, under clean and noisy observations. By progressively degrading the numerical evidence, we isolate when textual domain knowledge contributes beyond regression alone. Across the four systems, adding Text2Constraint reduces the worst form error from 1.397 to 0.499 on clean data and from 13.096 to 1.095 for data perturbed with Gaussian noise of $σ=0.1$; P2C also achieves lower form error and trajectory error than several LLM-based baselines over the tested noise range.
