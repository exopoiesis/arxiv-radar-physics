Quantum state tomography with measurement-conditioned diffusion models

Woohyun Choi, Changsuk Noh, Changhyoup Lee

arXiv:2611.19838·quant-ph·Published 2026-11-15

We introduce Diff-QST, a measurement-conditioned diffusion model for quantum state tomography that learns from an ensemble of input states and reconstructs a physical density operator from noisy, possibly incomplete Pauli measurements in two or three denoising steps. In simulations of two to five qubits, Diff-QST has higher mean fidelity than maximum-likelihood estimation (MLE) and a semidefinite-programming least-squares estimator (SDP) in all 120 tested conditions at nominal noise. After training, GPU reconstruction is faster than both in condition-averaged time at every system size and, at four and five qubits, in every condition. At four and five qubits, though not at two and three, Diff-QST also has higher fidelity than an architecture-matched regression baseline trained without diffusion. At five qubits, over the tested numbers of observables and shots per observable, it reaches a specified fidelity with 25%-67% fewer total shots than this baseline. Under state and noise distributions that differ from those used in training, Diff-QST retains its fidelity advantage over MLE and SDP in most tested conditions, although it can reverse. Diff-QST is therefore suited to fast tomography from noisy, incomplete Pauli measurements of states represented by its training ensemble.
