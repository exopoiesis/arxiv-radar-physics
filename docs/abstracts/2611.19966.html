Towards GPROF V08: A Machine-Learning Framework for Simulating Passive Microwave Observations of Precipitation

Simon Pfreundschuh, Christian D. Kummerow, Paula J. Brown

arXiv:2611.19966·physics.ao-ph·Published 2026-11-15

Spatially resolved precipitation retrievals based on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) can improve precipitation estimates from passive microwave (PMW) observations and will be used to implement the next operational PMW retrieval algorithm for the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) mission. However, the conventional radiative transfer simulations used to extend the retrieval algorithm to the various sensors of the GPM constellation do not provide the spatial coverage required to train CNN-based retrievals. To address this, we introduce a machine-learning-based simulation framework that enables the training of the CNN-based GPROF-NN 3D retrieval algorithm for the full GPM constellation. At the core of this framework is Satformer, a new neural-network architecture that can translate PMW observations between any pair of GPM sensors. The resulting synthetic observations exhibit lower errors than conventional radiative transfer simulations and lead to more accurate precipitation retrievals when used to train GPROF-NN precipitation retrievals. We further demonstrate the applicability of the framework to new instruments by developing GPROF-NN retrievals for the Microwave Imager aboard the Weather System Follow-on Microwave satellite. While GPROF-NN retrievals based on the new simulation framework achieve lower instantaneous retrieval errors and higher correlations compared to independent reference data than conventional retrievals, residual simulation errors can introduce systematic biases in the resulting retrievals. The proposed simulation framework provides a key component for extending GPROF-NN to the full GPM constellation as part of the upcoming operational GPROF V08 retrieval algorithm. The results demonstrate the potential of machine-learning-based simulations to support spatially resolved precipitation retrievals across heterogeneous satellite constellations.
