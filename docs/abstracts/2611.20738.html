Simultaneous tests of post-Newtonian inspiral using GW250114 and GW250207

Saurabh Kumar, Parthapratim Mahapatra, Ish Mohan Gupta, M. Saleem, B. S. Sathyaprakash, K. G. Arun

arXiv:2611.20738·gr-qc·Published 2026-11-16

We perform the one of the most general tests of post-Newtonian (PN) inspiral in binary black holes to date by allowing all eight phase coefficients up to 3.5PN order to vary simultaneously from their general-relativistic values. Using two of the loudest binary black hole events observed to date, GW250114 and GW250207, we carry out Bayesian inference with the IMRPhenomXPHM_SpinTaylor waveform model augmented by parametrized deviations in the post-Newtonian phasing. We then identify the combinations of phase coefficients most strongly constrained by the data through a principal component analysis of the eight-dimensional deformation space. We find that the data constrain the three leading principal components beyond their priors, and that all three are consistent with the predictions of general relativity. These results demonstrate that exceptionally loud gravitational-wave events enable robust, high-dimensional tests of inspiral dynamics and provide direct access to the measurable subspace of post-Newtonian deformations.
