Learning Morse Lyapunov functions through invertible neural networks

Wouter Jongeneel

arXiv:2611.21030·math.OC·Published 2026-11-16

Towards data-driven stability certificates of dynamical systems, we are witnessing renewed interest in studying and exploiting the structure of Lyapunov functions. Especially Lyapunov functions that are locally quadratic (i.e., Morse) gained popularity ever since it was understood that they can be written as the norm squared of a diffeomorphism. This topological representation alludes to parametrizations using invertible neural networks. In this note we discuss when and how such functions can be learned. In particular, using neural ODEs (NODEs), we characterize Sobolev approximation of these diffeomorphisms on compact sets and show how this naturally relates to stability. We also connect our results to PLNets and supply numerical experiments for illustration.
