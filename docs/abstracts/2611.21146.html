Model-based neural network for global floodplains analysis

Pierre Audisio, Barbara Belletti, Nelly Pustelnik

arXiv:2611.21146·cs.CV·Published 2026-11-16

We address the super-resolution of multispectral remote sensing imagery for long-term floodplain monitoring, enhancing Landsat acquisitions towards Sentinel-2 resolution. Exploiting the period during which both missions observed the same areas, we build DLhasa-L2S , to our knowledge the first paired dataset dedicated to multispectral super-resolution for river analysis. It covers the Lhasa River (Tibet), whose strong anthropogenic changes and diverse hydromorphological profiles make it an ideal study area. We developed two model-based neural networks: a forward-backward (FB) plug-and-play scheme and an unfolded FB scheme. We compared them against learned neural networks baselines (SwinIR, RDN) on synthetic data, on real scenes, and through a statistical test over the whole real paired Landsat-Sentinel dataset. The unfolded network achieves the highest PSNR and SSIM on the real RGB components with fast inference, offering the best trade-off between accuracy and computational cost. For NDWI-based water segmentation, its performance shows no statistically significant difference from that of the best methods.
