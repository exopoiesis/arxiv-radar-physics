Effects of QCD Equation of State on Cosmological Parameters and Stochastic Gravitational-Waves Spectrum

André V. Giannini, Victor P. Gonçalves, Samuel M. Sanches

arXiv:2611.22719·astro-ph.CO·Published 2026-11-17

We investigate the thermodynamic evolution of the early Universe across the QCD crossover and up to the electroweak (EW) scale, emphasizing the systematic impact of the QCD equation of state (EoS) and electroweak degrees of freedom on thermodynamic variables, cosmological parameters, and gravitational-wave observables. Using the HotQCD EoS as a baseline, we employ Gaussian Process (GP) regression to generate ensembles of 1000 thermodynamically consistent EoS samples, each extrapolated to high temperatures with and without EW contributions. For each ensemble, we solve the Friedmann equations to obtain the expansion history and compute gravitational-wave (GW) observables, including the instantaneous spectral tilt $n_{gw}(f)$ and the normalized GW energy density $Ω_{gw}(f)/Ω_{gw}^{\rm rad}$. We find that including the EW sector is the dominant effect across all observables, outweighing differences among QCD baseline models that share the same lattice calibration. In particular, EW contributions drive the EoS toward the radiation-dominated limit earlier, reduce excursions of the speed of sound toward the causality bound, suppress transient fluctuations in the Hubble and kinematic parameters near $t \sim 10^{-5}$ s, and qualitatively change the GW spectrum from a strongly frequency-dependent signal to a flat, suppressed plateau. These results demonstrate that accurate modeling of the electroweak sector is essential for an unbiased extraction of QCD-era physics from the stochastic gravitational-wave background.
