MRASLive: An online parameter estimation toolbox for time-dependent PDEs

Christian Aarset, Benedikt Kaltenbach, Tram Thi Ngoc Nguyen

arXiv:2611.23316·cs.MS·Published 2026-11-14

We present MRASLive, a toolbox for online parameter identification in time-dependent partial differential equations based on a model reference adaptive system (MRAS). Building on the MRAS proposed by Kaltenbacher and Nguyen (Inverse Problems 37(5) 2021) and its first numerical realization by the authors (Applied Mathematics for Modern Challenges 8 2026), the toolbox is designed for ease of use, utilising the user's weak form specification to prepare the data, the discretized MRAS and its solvers, and to verify the assumptions of MRAS's convergence theory semi-automatically.
