Reusing formal Vlasov-Maxwell proofs to certify plasma solver outputs

Bowen Zhu, Jian Wu

arXiv:2611.23319·physics.plasm-ph·Published 2026-11-15

AI now produces machine-checked mathematics faster than researchers can read it. Can such theorems do practical computational work? We reuse one from \mbox{OpenAI's} Lean~4 relativistic Vlasov--Maxwell library: initial fields determine the field at an outside point until plasma signals reach it, and the energy leaving a surrounding box before they reach its faces. New Lean proofs and numerical bounds give particle-free references for particle-in-cell simulations across three tasks. Domain size follows analytically from observation time, exposing boundary and initialization errors in WarpX and Smilei that global energy checks missed. Grid choice can be tested directly: combining two coarse grids recovers unknown plasma inputs within tolerance at a median 14.9-fold speedup over a fine grid, where the medium grid alone fails. For reduced models, the bounds establish the minimum number of time curves needed to predict energy loss.
