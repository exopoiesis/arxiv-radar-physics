Residual diffusion for fine-structure enhancement in low-count accelerator-based muon imaging

Haochen Wang, Pei Yu, Zhengyang He, Yuhong Yu, Xueheng Zhang, Liangwen Chen, Yu Zhang, Lei Yang, Zhiyu Sun

arXiv:2611.23776·physics.acc-ph·Published 2026-11-18

Accelerator-based muon imaging combines the penetrating power of high-energy muons with selectable momentum and a controlled beam direction, offering a powerful means of probing internal structures in large, dense and heavily shielded objects. Within a fixed beam-time budget, realizing this potential requires reliable recovery of fine structures from limited muon statistics. We propose an observation-conditioned residual diffusion method with decoupled linear schedules to enhance low-count muon images through iterative restoration guided by the observed image. We evaluate a simplified VSC-24 fuel cask under an idealized 5 GeV parallel muon beam, using 500,000 accepted events per low-count image and 40 million per reference image. Simulation results show improved recovery of structural boundaries and fine details, with the mean peak signal-to-noise ratio increasing from 11.4239 to 22.1719 dB. These findings highlight the potential of diffusion models to make more effective use of limited muon data and broaden the application of accelerator-based muon imaging across scientific and industrial settings.
