Pixel2Perspective: A Benchmark for Multi-Modal and Multi-View Generalization in Vision-Language Models

Aditya Gandhamal, Aniruddh Sikdar, Arya Kishor, Suresh Sundaram

arXiv:2611.24327·cs.CV·Published 2026-11-18

Embodied AI systems, such as drones and autonomous vehicles, improve navigation by enhancing perception in environments containing unseen objects. While Visual Language Models (VLMs) offer strong image understanding, their real-world application is underexplored. For effective deployment, open-vocabulary semantic segmentation (OVSS) models, leveraging models like CLIP to assign pixel-level labels from text, must be robust to outdoor conditions, adverse weather, and varying viewpoints. Despite their potential, these models encounter significant challenges in cross-domain generalization, hindering their practical use in embodied AI applications. In this work, we present Pixel2Perspective, a benchmark for multi-angle object localization and open-vocabulary segmentation across both aerial and ground perspectives. This benchmark supports thorough evaluation across different viewpoints and sensor modalities. We assess foundational models like CLIP and advanced OVSS models using a curated dataset that spans varied outdoor conditions, sim-to-real scenarios, and multiple sensors, including infrared, to support the development of robust and generalizable perception systems. We also present a novel framework, Multi-Source RGB-IR Uncertainty Quantification (MSRIQ), which jointly models aleatoric and epistemic uncertainties across RGB and infrared imagery. This approach supports robust evaluation of open-vocabulary segmentation models across diverse environmental conditions. Our work sets a robustness-driven benchmark, providing key insights and laying the foundation for future research in open-world embodied AI perception. The benchmark and project page are available at https://aniruddhsikdar1.github.io/publications/pixel2perspective/
