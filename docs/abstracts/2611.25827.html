Moiré-Enabled Recursive Phase Unwrapping for Differential Atom Interferometry

Junjie Jiang, Sitong Yan, Jiaming Wei, Chuan He, Xi Chen, Lin Zhou, Jin Wang, Ming-Sheng Zhan

arXiv:2611.25827·physics.atom-ph·Published 2026-11-19

Weak equivalence principle tests comparing different elements may be more sensitive to violations than those comparing isotopes. However, differences in effective wave vector complicate common-mode rejection of vibration noise. We propose a Moiré-enabled recursive phase unwrapping (MRPU) method for differential measurements with dual-species atom interferometers. The method exploits the moiré structure of the joint phase likelihood and temporal acceleration correlations to unwrap both phases and determine the relative acceleration. We implemented an $^{85}$Rb $\mathrm{D_1/D_2}$ line dual-scale-factor interferometer with an identical normalized transfer function and a large effective wave-vector difference to emulate different atomic species. Under $40\,\mathrm{rad}$ equivalent vibrational noise, this method achieved phase unwrapping over 5000 consecutive measurements, with a relative-acceleration Allan deviation of $1.47\times10^{-8}g$ at 997 shots. Numerical simulations under random vibrations and slow drifts show a $9.76 - 26.03\,\mathrm{dB}$ increase in the tolerable narrowband vibration amplitude. This method is promising for weak equivalence principle tests based on different atomic species, dark matter and dark energy detection, and high-dynamic-range atomic gravimetry.
