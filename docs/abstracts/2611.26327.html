Quantum Metric Control of Topological Superconductivity and Incommensurate Charge Density Wave in an Interacting Lieb-Kitaev Chain Model

Jiale Xuan, Grant Z. X. Yang, K. T. Law, Xiao Yan Xu

arXiv:2611.26327·cond-mat.str-el·Published 2026-11-19

We study an interacting spinless Lieb-Kitaev chain model in the nearly flat-band regime, motivated by the recent observation that Majorana zero modes in the noninteracting Lieb-Kitaev system inherit two decay modes: an ordinary Bogoliubov-de Gennes (BdG) length and a longer geometric length controlled by the quantum metric. By tuning both the interaction strength and quantum metric, we identify a topological superconducting (TSC) phase, an incommensurate charge-density-wave (ICDW)phase, and a commensurate charge-density-wave (CDW) phase. Inside the TSC phase, the QML is enhanced when increasing the interactions. In the ICDW phase, the interactions are modulated by quantum metric to generate an effective chemical potential, leading to a discontinuous incommensurate-to-commensurate (IC-C) transition. We verify these phases and conclusions using low energy effective theory and density-matrix renormalization group (DMRG) calculations.
