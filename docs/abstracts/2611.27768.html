Solving fermionic sign problems with physics-informed kernels

Kenji Fukushima, Friederike Ihssen, Renzo Kapust, Jan M. Pawlowski

arXiv:2611.27768·hep-lat·Published 2026-11-20

We extend the generative sampling approach with physics-informed kernels (PIKs) [1,2] to a general class of physics systems with complex distributions and a structure also encountered in fermionic systems. The resolution of sign problems of generic weighted sums of complex Gaußian distributions with the PIK approach is central to this application. In a first step, PIKs map a real normal distribution to such a weighted sum, and this procedure is called the PIKfan. The PIKfan has the sampling cost of a real normal distribution. In a second step, this weighted sum is evolved with a second PIK into the distribution of the theory at hand. In combination, we reduce the sampling task for a general class of complex systems to a Gaußian one, and the main computational task is that of computing the PIK. We illustrate the approach with the one-site Hubbard model at arbitrary chemical potential and with $φ^4$-deformed extensions of it.
