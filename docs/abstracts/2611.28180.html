Machine-learning-assisted photon pairing for neutral-pion reconstruction at future Higgs factories

Yi Wang, Chao Yang, Tianyi Yang, Enrui Tian, Yuexin Wang, Song Pan, Jun Guo, Manqi Ruan

arXiv:2611.28180·physics.ins-det·Published 2026-11-20

Neutral-pion ($π^0$) reconstruction is important for precision measurements at high-energy colliders, particularly in flavor, $τ$, and Higgs physics. We study $π^0$ candidate selection in four benchmark physics processes at electron--positron colliders, comparing an optimized diphoton mass-window (MW) baseline with four machine-learning methods: a boosted decision tree (BDT), a multilayer perceptron (MLP), ParticleNet (PN), and Particle Transformer (ParT). Performance is evaluated using the product of selection efficiency and purity. At the reference ECAL energy resolution, all four machine-learning methods outperform MW. PN and ParT achieve relative improvements of 53%--95% and 60%--128%, respectively, primarily through better rejection of incorrectly matched photon pairs. Their performance advantage is maintained across the investigated ECAL energy resolutions. Training-sample-size scans reveal two periods of rapid performance improvement separated by slower growth in most PN and ParT learning curves. The improved candidate selection provides a starting point for further studies of $ρ$ and hadronic $τ$ reconstruction.
