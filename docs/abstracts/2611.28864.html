Learning the Boltzmann Collision Operator with Structural Constraints for Fast Kinetic Simulation

Hao Wang, Zhenning Cai

arXiv:2611.28864·math.NA·Published 2026-11-21

The high-dimensional integration in the Boltzmann collision operator is a major computational bottleneck in deterministic kinetic simulations. We develop a structure-preserving neural operator that provides an efficient approximation of this nonlinear collision mapping. The operator maintains the gain-loss structure of the original Boltzmann collision operator, and, by construction, is proportional to the square of the gas density. Conservative moment projection and entropy correction enforce conservation of mass, momentum, and energy, as well as entropy dissipation. By construction, the collision term also vanishes at all Maxwellians. In the numerical scheme for simulations of gas dynamics, we establish nonnegativity of the distribution function with the learned collision operator and prove exponential relaxation to the moment-matched equilibrium when a positive spectral gap is imposed. Numerical experiments for a constant collision kernel demonstrate a favorable accuracy-cost tradeoff relative to Fourier spectral evaluations. Although trained on smooth distributions, the same operator handles discontinuous distribution functions and is embedded without retraining in steady Couette flow, normal-shock, and two-dimensional lid-driven cavity solvers. These results demonstrate a reusable approximation for repeated collision evaluations that retains kinetic structure and supports accurate deterministic simulation.
