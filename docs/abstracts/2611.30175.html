Observable-Level Expressivity in Fixed-Readout Quantum Neural Networks

Iqra Bashir, Yuanfeng Wang, Mingda Huang, Keren Li, Qi Qin

arXiv:2611.30175·quant-ph·Published 2026-11-23

A central question in quantum machine learning is which functions of an input state a model can represent when its measurement is fixed by hardware or by experimental design. We address this question for quantum neural networks with a fixed readout observable, where the trainable pre-measurement dynamics determine the effective observable in the Heisenberg picture and the scalar output is the measured expectation value itself, without trainable affine post-processing. We characterize the reachable effective observables under three physical resource classes: unitary evolution, which preserves the spectrum of the readout; mixed-unitary evolution, which relaxes this to a majorization constraint while preserving the trace; and completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) evolution, whose Heisenberg adjoint is unital rather than trace-preserving and therefore lifts the trace constraint inherited at the mixed-unitary level. The three reachable sets are a fixed-spectrum orbit, its convex hull, and an operator interval, and the inclusions are strict for any readout that is not proportional to the identity. The separation is witnessed locally: for a fixed Pauli readout $Z_j$ on an arbitrary number of qubits, the amplitude-damping observable $γ\id+(1-γ)Z_j$ lies outside the mixed-unitary level for every $γ>0$. We realize the CPTP level with a Stinespring-channelized circuit and derive closed-form loss floors, $4γ^2/3$ and $γ^2$ for the unitary and mixed-unitary levels and zero at the CPTP level, which the numerics reproduce within precision. Structured-observable, Rényi-moment and classification experiments illustrate how the hierarchy manifests in finite-depth ansätze. The results identify which spectral constraint each physical resource relaxes, framing reachable-observable classes as a resource for expressivity rather than an architectural advantage.
