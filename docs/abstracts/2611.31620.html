Bayesian Data Integration and Model Updating in Probabilistic Digital Twins

Dafydd Cotoarbă, Antonis Mavritsakis, Timo Schweckendiek, Ian FC Smith, Daniel Straub

arXiv:2611.31620·stat.AP·Published 2026-11-23

This paper proposes a framework for sequential Bayesian updating in probabilistic digital twins using data from multiple sources collected over time. The framework distinguishes between property data and behavior data, according to how observations relate to the digital state. Property data directly inform a subset of the uncertain model parameters and can therefore be incorporated using efficient dedicated Bayesian updating methods. Behavior data provide indirect information through the prediction model and require the solution of a Bayesian inverse problem. To reduce the associated computational cost, an active-learning-based surrogate strategy is introduced that evolves with the digital state and adaptively refines the prediction model in regions relevant to posterior inference. The framework is evaluated using an idealized cantilever beam example and a geotechnical case study of a canal wall subject to varying water levels and corrosion. The examples show how complementary data sources, including both equality observations and inequality information from observed system survival, can inform uncertain parameters and system reliability. The proposed surrogate-based updating scheme yields posterior estimates and prediction accuracy comparable to those of physics-based updating while requiring substantially fewer model evaluations. The proposed framework thus supports multi-source updating and reliability assessment in probabilistic digital twins of engineering systems.
