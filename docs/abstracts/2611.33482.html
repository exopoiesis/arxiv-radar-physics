Physics-informed neural networks for quark Dyson--Schwinger equations and propagator singularities

Xin-Peng Li, Zhong-Hua Huang

arXiv:2611.33482·hep-ph·Published 2026-11-24

We develop a physics-informed neural-network (PINN) framework for solving the rainbow-truncated quark Dyson--Schwinger equation at real and complex momenta. The resulting dressing functions agree closely with conventional iterative solutions. We investigate the singularity structure of the quark propagator using two complementary methods. Analytic continuation of a PINN trained on the real momentum axis estimates the locations of poles and further reveals three complex-conjugate pole pairs. An independent reconstruction using PINN-parametrized spectral densities and a single pole pair yields $q=-0.146\pm0.391i~\mathrm{GeV}^2$. The agreement between the two methods substantially strengthens confidence in the properties of the leading singularities. Both spectral densities change sign, and the negative regions of the vector density and the complex poles indicate a violation of reflection positivity, consistent with quark confinement. Finally, we use the extracted poles and residues to calculate their contributions to two order parameters at finite quark chemical potential, the chiral condensate and the quark number density.
