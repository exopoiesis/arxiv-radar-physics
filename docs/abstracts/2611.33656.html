Online Identification Can Replace a Floor-Plan Prior on Unseen Buildings

Elias T. -H. Shieh, Chun Fu

arXiv:2611.33656·eess.SY·Published 2026-11-25

The Smart Buildings Challenge asks for one HVAC control policy that runs office buildings and weather it has never seen, from the building's metadata and sensor streams alone. Our controller sees no data from the target building before it runs and is not tuned per building; its few global constants were set on development scenarios. Each zone has a first-order grey-box model that starts from coefficients computed from the floor plan and is updated online by recursive least squares, and a planner and a closed-form mapping turn the models into plant commands. On two sets of buildings and weather that we generated, a validation set and a stress set with real-year weather, it scores 0.145 and 0.159 times the starter kit's PID baseline, and 0.095 times on a holdout built after all versions were fixed and scored once. It improves on every building, also against our two implementations of ASHRAE Guideline 36: per set it scores 0.60 to 0.71 times a basic version and 0.83 to 0.96 times one partly tuned on the public leaderboard, which wins one of 54 scenarios; with a 5 °C frost target for empty zones on both, the margin over the tuned version shrinks to 1.4% on the stress set, where one of six buildings is 0.2% worse. In an ablation on a reduced-order model, removing both the floor-plan prior and online identification costs 28%, 20% and 42% on the validation, stress and holdout sets, far more than the two effects combined: either source of building-specific information recovers most of what is lost without both. On one office floor in a second simulator, BOPTEST, this does not carry over: with identification on, the two priors give similar results, but the floor-plan prior alone, with constants fitted on the competition's simulator, is the least stable arm. These simulation results motivate testing floor-plan-initialised adaptive control in buildings that have drawings but no operating data.
