Simultaneous Inversion of Drift and Potential in the Fractional Magnetic Schrödinger Equation

Yun-Long Liu, Jin Wen, Jun-Liang Fu, Xue-Juan Ren

arXiv:2611.34189·math.NA·Published 2026-11-25

We study the Calderón problem for the fractional magnetic Schrödinger equation with drift in the bivariate additive framework. When the magnetic potential $A$ is known and fixed, we prove that the drift $b$ and the scalar potential $q$ can be simultaneously and uniquely determined from exterior Dirichlet-to-Neumann measurements. When $A$ is unknown, we prove that the triple $(A,b,q)$ can only be recovered up to a natural gauge equivalence class, and that the drift $b$ is a gauge invariant, hence uniquely determined. The proofs rely on the Alessandrini identity, Runge approximation, and the unique continuation property for fractional magnetic operators. Furthermore, we have developed a numerical reconstruction framework that combines finite difference discretization, radial basis function surrogate modeling, and smoothed TV regularization. One-dimensional and two-dimensional numerical examples have verified the effectiveness of the proposed method and supported the theoretical uniqueness result.
